## ③ ⛔⛔ THE INSTRUMENT FINDING: THE END MARKER REPORTS THE PROBE, NOT THE CELL

Four cells are logged by the chain as `end=PERSIST-INDETERMINATE phase-1 the subject gave NO answer
on the final turn, so persistence could not be read (not a half-life measurement)`. That sentence is

⇒ ***A READER OF THE CHAIN LOG CANNOT SEE THAT THESE CELLS WERE KILLED.*** The marker's own
parenthesis — *"not a half-life measurement"* — reads as reassurance about a probe while the cell
behind it did not land. The relight order I was handed quoted exactly these three log lines, and I
read them the same way for the first twenty minutes of this shift. **The other 34 fired cells all
carry `done_reason LANDED` and `landed True`, so the eleven previously-scored conditions are
unaffected; this is a reporting defect, not a data defect.**

**THE CAUSE, NAMED AT THE OBJECT.** All four cells' stderr carries
`Post "https://oauth2.googleapis.com/token": Forbidden`. **Not model slowness — a refused credential
refresh**, after which the client sat until the 1800s per-turn print timeout expired.
⛔ **IT IS ARM-CORRELATED — 4 of 4 are salt-diet, 0 of 18 control cells — AND THE MECHANISM IS
DURATION, NOT TREATMENT.** The control cells finish in 147–678s and never straddle a token refresh;
the treated cells run 670–5,539s and do. ⇒ 🔑 ***A CREDENTIAL WINDOW IS A PER-TURN CAP THAT NOBODY
DECLARED, AND EXPOSURE TO IT IS PROPORTIONAL TO HOW LONG A CELL RUNS — SO IT IS ARM-CORRELATED BY
CONSTRUCTION, EXACTLY LIKE THE CAPS §3 ALREADY NAMES.*** This is a new member of a known class.
✅ **And its sign here runs AGAINST the simple story, which is why it is worth stating:** the two
Paxos cells that were cut off scored **17/17**, full marks. **Being cut off is not the same as
failing**, and a reader who treats `landed=False` as a failure would have discarded the treatment's
two best Paxos cells.

statement about a probe. **The parenthesis is what does the damage, because it answers a question
about scope and a reader who has been answered stops asking.** No rewording removes that; a field the
marker cannot speak for does.

**Driven in three directions against real cells on disk, using the line EXTRACTED FROM THE INSTALLED
