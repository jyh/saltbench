TURN DISTRIBUTION, and whether it can price stage C's cap
  n=10  cap=40  median=33.5  max=40
  episodes AT the cap: 2 of 10 (20%)
  p90 of calls: 40

  ⛔ THE DISTRIBUTION IS CENSORED AT THE CAP: 2 of 10 episodes stopped BECAUSE of it, so every
     observation at 40 is a LOWER BOUND on what that episode wanted. A p90 computed from this
     data cannot exceed 40 — measuring a cap from data the cap produced returns the cap.
  ⇒ stage C's cap needs turns-to-first-rc0 from an UNCENSORED read, or a cap raised until the
     censoring fraction is ~0 and THEN measured.
